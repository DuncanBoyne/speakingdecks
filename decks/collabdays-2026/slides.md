Notes:
“Don't fix it yet” IS the prompt. Without it the model rewrites your measure and you learn nothing. Highest-value prompt in the pack for anyone past beginner - if the room is senior, spend the extra minute here.

---

<!-- .slide: data-background-image="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DuncanBoyne/speakingdecks/main/decks/collabdays-2026/slides/09.png" data-background-size="contain" data-background-color="#060A0D" -->

Notes:
Show of hands: who has inherited a measure they hate? That earns the next ninety seconds. “The change-list is the documentation you never wrote” is the line people quote back to you afterwards.
